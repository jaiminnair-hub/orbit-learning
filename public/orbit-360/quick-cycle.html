Who is this about?

This is the person receiving confidential feedback. Only people already in your private workforce directory can be picked here — if they're missing, add them via People in the full builder first.

2

Who is reviewing?

Reviewers can come from your own directory, a whole department at once, someone outside your organisation by email, or a spreadsheet. The subject can never review themselves — Orbit removes them automatically if they end up in this list. Nothing is sent to anyone until you approve and launch in step 5.

Search the directory, pick a whole department, add someone by email, or upload a list.

Excel or CSV with two columns: Full Name and Email. One row per reviewer — no need to repeat the subject, that's already set in step 1.

3

What should they be asked?

AI can draft a starting set of questions based on the purpose you pick — it's told the purpose and a generic department, never who the subject actually is, so it can produce a weak question at worst, never a privacy mistake. Edit, remove, or replace anything it suggests before moving on.

Pick a purpose, then let AI draft a starting set of questions — review, edit, or regenerate before anything is saved. This is the only step that uses AI, and it never sees names.

4

When, and how private?

The anonymity threshold decides how many reviewers must respond before a group's ratings and comments become visible in the report. Below that number, individual answers stay withheld — even from you as the administrator — so a small reviewer group can never be traced back to one person.

5

Review & launch

Nothing above has been sent or made live yet — everything so far is a private draft only you can see. Clicking launch below creates the cycle, sets it live, and sends a private invitation link to every reviewer listed above. This is the one irreversible step.

6

Manage & release reports

A report can only be released after its cycle is closed — closing stops new responses so what you release can't change afterward. Below the anonymity threshold, a group's scores and comments stay withheld here too, exactly as they do everywhere else in Orbit.

Pick any cycle — including ones you launched earlier — to close it and release its report.
